About Me

Turning ideas into working software

I'm a Software Developer with a National Diploma in Information Technology from Bulawayo Polytechnic. I work as a Software Developer Assistant with Triplepower Engineering, outsourced to different client companies, and I also teach PHP.

Being placed with different companies means I adapt quickly to new teams, codebases and business problems. My day-to-day involves building web and mobile information systems end to end — analysing what users need, designing the database, developing features in PHP, Laravel, React and Flutter, integrating REST APIs, then testing, debugging and preparing them for deployment.

I'm most interested in web and mobile development: products people actually use every day, like the point-of-sale system I designed and deployed from scratch. Before development, I spent a year in IT support at NUST, which taught me to think about the person on the other side of the screen.
